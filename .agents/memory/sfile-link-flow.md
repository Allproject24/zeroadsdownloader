The expected route is `sfl.gl` → `app.khaddavi.net`: click Open and wait 10 seconds, click Next, scroll to and click the next Open, then continue on the second `app.khaddavi.net` article (which may open in a new tab), wait 3 seconds after Continue, and click Go to link. Continue through `sfl.gl/ready/go` and Open link to `safefileku.com`; wait for its file-ready countdown, then click Download. Unrelated external ad tabs stay passive; same-site article tabs remain part of the download flow. The destination article can vary between visits, and a button transition may appear about one second after the stated wait, so rescan briefly before declaring the page unsupported. Requeue same-site destination pages so their countdown or verification state is inspected before searching for another button. Capture validated Safefileku URLs and inspect human-verification state across flow frames; otherwise a URL observed in a nested frame can remain hidden when the job fails. Keep verification manual and expose only the observed official `/download/<id>` URL.

**Why:** The user provided the actual sequence used in 1DM, including background-tab behavior. Live checks showed the article destination can rotate, “Next” can appear just after the 10-second wait, and Safefileku may present a Cloudflare human-verification checkpoint. Navigation capture already includes child frames, but checking only the top-level page can miss a nested challenge and suppress the manual destination link.

**How to apply:** Use this route and timing when changing Sfile link-following behavior. Poll briefly for delayed controls, inspect newly reached pages for countdowns, and keep Cloudflare human verification as a distinct blocked checkpoint rather than attempting to solve or skip it. If Safefileku blocks the server session, expose only a validated manual link to its official `/download/<id>` page and state that the user will verify and download there; do not imply the server job can resume.
